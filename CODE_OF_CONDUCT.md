# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Scribe of Eras welcomes contributors and participants with a wide range of backgrounds, experience levels, and perspectives. We want project spaces to remain constructive, accessible, and focused on the work.

We are committed to providing a welcoming environment regardless of age, disability, appearance, body size, race, ethnicity, national origin, religion, sex, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, socioeconomic status, education, or level of experience.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful, considerate, and constructive.
- Critiquing ideas and work rather than attacking people.
- Explaining disagreements with evidence and clear reasoning.
- Welcoming questions and helping newcomers find context.
- Acknowledging uncertainty, mistakes, and differing interpretations.
- Respecting that historical subjects can involve contested evidence and painful experiences.
- Giving feedback that is specific, relevant, and actionable.

## Unacceptable behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, intimidation, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory, degrading, or hateful language.
- Sexualized comments or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Deliberate disruption, trolling, or repeated bad-faith engagement.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Retaliation against someone for raising a concern in good faith.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces, including GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, code review, and project-related public communication when someone is representing or participating in the project.

## Reporting

Report conduct that violates this policy to the project maintainer through GitHub's private contact channels. Do not post sensitive details publicly. Include links, dates, relevant context, and any supporting material you can safely provide.

The maintainer will review reports, respect privacy as far as practical, and determine an appropriate response. Responses may include a warning, removal of content, temporary restrictions, or exclusion from project spaces. The response will depend on the circumstances and severity.
