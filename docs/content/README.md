# Historical and Content Documentation

This directory contains authored-content standards and historical research/content specifications. These materials support the GDD but do not override its design authority.

## Shared authoring standard

- [Content and Scenario Quality Standards](CONTENT_AND_SCENARIO_STANDARDS.md) — historical coherence, internal consistency, evidence coverage, human context, consequences, and character-situated interpretation.

## Era I historical research

The following Era I documents are maintained in the developer research workspace pending any later content-package promotion. They are research and design evidence, not runtime content or automatically approved game canon.

- [Era I Institutional and Administrative Reality — Historical Audit](../../dev/ERA_I_INSTITUTIONAL_HISTORICAL_AUDIT.md) — chronology, evidentiary limits, political/institutional landscape, and caution against unsupported modern categories.
- [Era I Institution and Responsibility Matrix](../../dev/ERA_I_INSTITUTION_RESPONSIBILITY_MATRIX.md) — confidence rules, institutional contexts, cross-path responsibilities, scenario seeds, and character-creation implications.

## Promotion rule

A research note or proposal becomes an authoritative content specification only after its evidence, confidence, owner decisions, and unresolved assumptions are clearly identified. Promotion into this directory does not by itself approve gameplay mechanics or change the GDD.
