# Era I Assignment Portfolio — Focused Historical Research Brief

**Status:** RESEARCH REQUEST — concise design input, not approved content  
**Period:** Uruk, c. 3200 BCE (Late Uruk / Uruk IV–III transition)  
**Workstream:** GD-OPPORTUNITY-001, Track 1  
**Goal:** Deliver playable, historically defensible Era I variety quickly. This is not a monograph.

## 1. Scope and priority

Prepare a concise shortlist of institution-anchored Assignment seeds for one shared pool, aiming for roughly two credible anchors per orientation context within the overall 8–9 bespoke-definition portfolio.

Priority:
1. **Commercial:** distinct exchange, procurement, counterparty, transport, receipt, custody, or delivery settings. The existing Storehouse receiving-account task is not sufficient coverage.
2. **Martial (high caution):** only defensible security, organized movement, logistics, custody, or accountability situations. Do not invent a standing army or military bureaucracy.
3. **Civic / Religious:** distinguish practical responsibilities and stakes while acknowledging overlapping institutions, personnel, resources, and cultic/economic activity.
4. **Recurring occurrences:** identify repeatable institutional needs and coherent parameter domains for counterparty, commodity/unit, discrepancy, and setting. These must support genuine new occurrences, not reskinned copies.

The existing three Temple Storehouse activities are the comparison baseline, not the required template. The approved orientation audit and institutional responsibility matrix are design inputs, not historical evidence.

## 2. Required output — short and usable

Return a compact research note with:

### A. Anchor seeds
Aim for 1–3 viable seeds per orientation context, but do not force two Martial seeds if the evidence cannot support them. For each seed provide:
- short working title and institutional setting;
- human/operational problem and likely participants;
- what a junior scribe could actually do, and who retains authority;
- evidence/record types and plausible consequence;
- why it is meaningfully distinct from existing Storehouse work;
- one or two stable source citations;
- confidence label and one-sentence limitation.

### B. Recurrence parameter table
A compact table listing compatible combinations of:
- counterparty / institutional relationship;
- commodity and appropriate unit;
- source and receiving evidence or other discrepancy;
- setting / custody transition;
- institutional purpose / recipient.

Mark each combination **Attested**, **Inferred**, or **PROVISIONAL**. List only useful valid combinations and important invalid combinations. Do not create universal units or arbitrary cross-products. A discrepancy must have a defensible evidence basis.

### C. Red flags
A short list of tempting anachronisms or claims the team must not make, especially for Martial institutions and Civic/Religious classification.

## 3. Confidence labels

- **Attested:** directly supported for the relevant place and period by identified evidence. Cite the tablet/object/context or precise publication reference.
- **Inferred:** a reasoned interpretation from evidence, but the specific workflow, office, role, or event is not directly attested.
- **PROVISIONAL:** a bounded game reconstruction proposed to make a playable situation. State what is reconstructed and do not present it as historical fact.

Separate source observation from interpretation and game reconstruction. Note chronology/phase uncertainty where it changes the claim. Do not use later-period parallels as direct proof for Uruk c. 3200 BCE.

## 4. Historical guardrails

- Do not assume a modern division between state and church or a single unified temple bureaucracy.
- Do not assume later private merchant houses or a standardized merchant profession.
- Do not present a standing army, conscription system, muster rolls, military scribal corps, quartermaster office, regiment, or standardized military ration system as established Uruk IV practice without direct evidence.
- Do not use the later Uruk city wall as proof of a c. 3200 BCE military system; date defensive evidence independently.
- Do not infer an office, procedure, reading, or actor's identity merely from an object/sign or room label.
- Civic and Religious seeds may share people and institutions; distinguish them through purpose, recipients, responsibility, evidence, and consequences.

## 5. Handoff

Deliver the anchor shortlist, recurrence table, red flags, citations, and unresolved questions in a concise issue comment or linked note. Game Design will select and author the playable scenarios. No Production work is requested.

**Fastest useful result:** prioritize one defensible Commercial seed, the strongest possible Martial seed(s), and one Civic/Religious distinction set; then expand only where evidence supports it.
