## 1. Work premise and scale

The Eanna Work is the required Era I milestone Work, **The Eanna Provisioning Undertaking**. Its complete scope is the four-stage compilation and official disposition of one Consolidated Summary Tablet: separate household/precinct source tallies on the obverse, compatible category totals and shortfall allocation on the reverse, then Lu-Nanshe's authorized disposition.

It is not a longer stock audit. The Work asks the apprentice to make a reliable account out of material that does not all have the same status: written tallies, physical counts, and spoken requests or expectations. The player must decide what can be carried onto the tablet, what must remain separate, and what cannot yet be counted. The official—not the apprentice—decides the allocation.

The approaching Eanna gathering gives the work consequence. Its game-calendar window is Days 35–38, with the gathering on Day 39; this timing and gathering are explicitly **authored reconstruction**, not an attested festival date.

The apprentice begins without office, formal authority, or a personal seal. They compile, check, and submit. The authorized official reviews the proposal, decides whether to ratify or alter it, and applies the institutional seal impression where the authored closeout uses one.

**Historical confidence:** Eanna is a major, well-documented Late Uruk administrative/cultic precinct associated with extensive archaic administrative material. The exact gathering, named participants' involvement in this particular undertaking, source packet, consolidated two-view tablet, allocation problem, and four-stage sequence are playable reconstruction—not a documented historical event or standardized Eanna procedure.

## 2. Core design rules

- The two-view Summary Tablet workspace is exclusive to **The Eanna Work**. Routine Assignments remain lightweight and snappy.
- Front / Obverse: individual household/precinct source tallies, retaining their source identity, commodity/category, quantity, and own unit/notation.
- Back / Reverse: consolidated category totals only where comparison is valid; otherwise keep categories separate. Include the available amount and provisional shortfall treatment only where the source state establishes them.
- Do not aggregate unlike commodities, numerical systems, or labor counts into a false grand total.
- The clay tablet remains visible as the work surface. Dialogue appears over or around it. Player actions create the marks.
- Use existing accessible React panel patterns and clay UI tokens `--surface-card` and `--surface-pressed`; lightweight SVG/visual motifs may accompany accessible text.
- No bespoke sign-decoding engine, custom font/editor, canvas-only interaction, unreadable symbol quiz, new art dependency, or wireframe blocker.
- The tablet itself carries concise accounting marks—categories, quantities, totals, and appropriate seal impression—not spoken arguments, multi-clause explanations, legal caveats, or modern report prose.
- A shortfall is not proof of guilt or misconduct. An assurance is not a physical count. A request is not an entitlement. A sealing is not proof that the holder had authority to commit a load.
- Distinguish physical reality, the source Record, the new Summary Tablet, and what each person or institution knows.
- The apprentice's provisional allocation layout is a recommendation for review, not an order or authorization. Only the appointed official makes the institutional disposition.

## 4. Source packet and quantity discipline

All quantities below are **fresh, gathering-specific authored reconstruction** created for this Work on 25 November 2026. They are not transcriptions of excavated tablets and are not borrowed from COM-02/CIV-02. They create a legible allocation problem while preserving separate commodity and unit contexts.

### Authored source packet

| Source / claimant | Commodity and quantity | Source status | What the number establishes |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Eanna storehouse tally, reported by Ur-Nanna | **18 barley capacity measures available** | Authored physical-count reconstruction | Amount counted as available for this gathering; excludes later arrivals |
| Nin-kasi / brewing-house tally | **12 barley capacity measures requested** | Authored written-tally reconstruction | Brewing-house requirement for the gathering |
| Geme-Inana / textile-household tally | **9 barley capacity measures requested** | Authored written-tally reconstruction | Household work-group request for the gathering |
| Gathering bread-preparation tally | **8 barley capacity measures requested** | Authored written-tally reconstruction; purpose supplied by Lu-Nanshe's instruction | Third claim on the same barley category and capacity unit |
| Inana-associated offering preparation | **6 discrete beer vessels requested; 4 vessels counted as prepared** | Authored offering/count reconstruction | Separate vessel count and shortfall; vessels are not converted into barley |
| Enlil-kisu's spoken account | **3 barley capacity measures expected after Day 39** | Reported expectation only; no arrival count or receipt | Possible later supply. Not included in the 18 physically counted and not entered as present stock |

The three competing barley claims total **29 capacity measures** against **18 counted available**, leaving an **11-measure shortfall** before allocation. The offering preparation separately requests **6 beer vessels** against **4 counted prepared**, leaving **2 vessels**. These category totals are never added together.

**Barley capacity measure** is a readable scenario unit label, not a claim to identify a particular ancient sign, named metrological unit, or modern volume. The vessel figure is a discrete object count, not a capacity conversion. Use verified Era I notation where available; otherwise show a neutral mark and accessible modern-language label rather than fabricated Proto-Cuneiform.

### Stage 1 sorting rule

The player sorts material before entering the tablet:

1. **Written tallies / copied counts:** the three barley requests and the separate vessel count; eligible for transcription with source and category preserved.
2. **Physical/storehouse counts:** 18 barley capacity measures and 4 prepared vessels; each remains tied to its own counted category.
3. **Spoken expectation:** Enlil-kisu's expected later 3 barley capacity measures; dialogue context only, not present stock.

This is the distinct Stage 1 judgment: which items are countable now, which are separate categories, and which remain spoken expectations. Stage 2 transcribes eligible figures; it does not repeat the sorting exercise.

### Tablet discipline

The obverse carries source-linked categories and quantities only. The reverse carries the compatible barley total, barley allocation/shortfall, and separate beer-vessel count/disposition. No dialogue, explanations, accusations, legal caveats, or prose clauses appear on the clay. Human arguments remain spoken around the work.
## 5. Four-stage playable scenario
## 5. Four-stage playable scenario

### Stage 1 — Sort the sources and establish what can be counted

**Purpose:** Establish the scope of the compilation and distinguish record-backed quantities from separate counts and spoken claims.

**Player sees:**
- Written tallies: 12 barley capacity measures requested by Nin-kasi; 9 by Geme-Inana; 8 by the gathering bread-preparation group.
- Ur-Nanna's storehouse count of 18 barley capacity measures and the separate count of 4 prepared beer vessels.
- The Inana-associated offering request for 6 discrete beer vessels.
- Enlil-kisu's spoken expectation that 3 further barley capacity measures may arrive after Day 39; no arrival receipt exists.
- The Days 35–38 compilation window and Day 39 gathering.

**Player actions:**
- Sort each item as a written tally/copy, separate count/category, or spoken claim/assurance.
- Identify which entries can be carried forward and which must remain separate.
- Identify incompatible commodity categories or numerical contexts before calculation.
- Preserve the source identity and provenance; do not promote an oral assurance to a recorded quantity.

**Dialogue:**
- Shuruppak-ilu explains that the task is to prepare a usable account, not decide who deserves goods.
- Ur-Nanna distinguishes a storehouse count from a statement about goods expected later.
- Nin-kasi and Geme-Inana each explain what delay means for their work.
- Enlil-kisu distinguishes what he dispatched or reported from what has actually arrived.
- Lu-Nanshe makes clear that the priority decision remains hers.

**Stage output:** A source-sorted working set identifying 18 barley measures and 4 prepared vessels as counted availability, the three distinct barley claims, the offering-vessel request, and Enlil-kisu's 3-measure expectation as uncounted future supply.

### Stage 2 — Compile the obverse / front

**Purpose:** Enter the separate source tallies without erasing their origins or units.

**Player actions:**
- Select each eligible source tally and enter its commodity/category and quantity.
- Preserve each source's own unit and numerical context.
- Keep separate household/precinct entries distinct even when they concern related goods.
- Review for omissions, transpositions, or accidental mixing of units.

Each player entry visibly adds a mark or lightweight visual motif to the clay surface. Every mark is paired with readable accessible text. The player does not draw ancient signs by hand or decode the visual motif.

**Dialogue:**
- Nin-kasi may correct how a category is described, but her statement does not silently change the source tally.
- Enlil-kisu may clarify whether he is describing dispatch, expectation, or receipt.
- Geme-Inana may press for her household work group's barley share; the spoken argument does not change the written request tally.

**Stage output:** Obverse/front entries show 12, 9, and 8 barley capacity measures as three separate claims, plus the separate request for 6 beer vessels and count of 4 prepared vessels. No unlike category is combined.

### Stage 3 — Consolidate the reverse and prepare a provisional allocation

**Purpose:** Calculate compatible totals and lay out a proposed treatment for the shortfall for Lu-Nanshe's review.

**Player actions:**
- Turn to the reverse/back view.
- Consolidate only entries whose category and units are demonstrably comparable.
- Consolidate the three barley claims: 12 + 9 + 8 = **29 barley capacity measures requested** against **18 counted available**, leaving an **11-measure shortfall**.
- Keep the beer-vessel category separate: **6 requested**, **4 prepared**, **2 short**.
- Do not add barley measures to beer vessels or include Enlil-kisu's expected 3 measures in present stock.
- Prepare one provisional allocation layout for Lu-Nanshe's review.
- Attribute any carry-forward or expected delivery to the person/source that made the claim; do not add it to physical stock.

**The apprentice's decision:** Choose which provisional barley split and vessel shortfall treatment to place before Lu-Nanshe. The default recommendation is 10 barley measures for Nin-kasi, 5 for the bread-preparation group, and 3 for Geme-Inana; 4 prepared beer vessels for the offering. This uses all 18 counted barley measures and all 4 prepared vessels, leaving shortfalls of 2, 3, and 6 barley measures respectively, plus 2 vessels. Other defensible splits are possible. The player is not choosing an enforceable allocation.

**Official authority:** Lu-Nanshe may ratify the proposal, change the priority, defer a portion, or reject the proposal. She alone makes the institutional allocation decision in this scenario. Shuruppak-ilu supervises the record and may explain why a proposal exceeds the evidence, but he does not choose the beneficiary.

**Dialogue:**
- Nin-kasi argues that a late or reduced allocation will affect the work already underway.
- Geme-Inana points out that a reduced barley share leaves fewer people provisioned for the household work.
- Ur-Nanna states what is available under the storehouse record, without treating a request as an entitlement.
- Lu-Nanshe asks for the shortfall to remain visible rather than hidden in a total.

**Stage output:** A provisional Reverse layout for official review, showing category totals, the proposed 18-measure barley split, and the separate 4-vessel disposition. It is not an order, release instruction, or completed physical distribution.

### Stage 4 — Check, submit, and receive institutional disposition

**Purpose:** Complete the apprentice's record and pass the allocation decision to the authorized official.

**Player actions:**
- Review both tablet views.
- Check source tallies, unit separation, compatible totals, and the provisional allocation/shortfall notation.
- Identify the apprentice as compiler in the authored, historically appropriate way if required.
- Submit the tablet to Lu-Nanshe for disposition.

**Official action:**
- Lu-Nanshe reviews the source distinctions and provisional layout.
- She may ratify it, alter the priority, defer part of the distribution, or return it for correction.
- If the scenario's closeout uses an institutional seal impression, Lu-Nanshe applies it after her decision. The apprentice never applies the seal.
- Shuruppak-ilu may explain the assessment and recordkeeping lesson, but does not substitute his judgment for Lu-Nanshe's allocation authority.

**Outcome distinctions:**
- **Submitted:** the apprentice has completed and handed over the compilation.
- **Accepted / disposed:** Lu-Nanshe has made the institutional decision.
- **Institutionally sealed:** the authorized closeout is represented by the appropriate institutional seal impression, if used in this scenario.
- **Physical state:** the amount actually available, any delivery not yet received, and any unmet need remain as the underlying events establish. A carry-forward does not create stock.

**Stage output:** A committed Summary Tablet and official disposition. Later corrections or supplementary records preserve the earlier submission and disposition; they do not silently rewrite them.

## 7. Seasonal window, durable outcomes, and aftermath

### Explicit authored calendar window

- **Compilation window:** Era I Days **35–38**, inclusive.
- **Gathering:** scheduled for **Day 39**.
- **Decision deadline:** Lu-Nanshe's allocation disposition should be recorded by the end of Day 38 for the full planned gathering scope.

This is an explicit game-calendar window and an **authored reconstruction**, not an attested Uruk festival date or historical seasonal calendar. It is a world-date consequence, not a real-time timer or AP countdown.

### If the window is met

Lu-Nanshe receives the tablet by the end of Day 38 and makes the allocation decision before the gathering. The default authored disposition is:

- **Barley:** 10 capacity measures to Nin-kasi's brewing-house work; 5 to the gathering bread-preparation group; 3 to Geme-Inana's household work group. Total allocated: 18. The respective shortfalls are 2, 3, and 6 capacity measures.
- **Beer vessels:** 4 prepared vessels to the Inana-associated offering preparation; 2 requested vessels remain unmet.

This is the concrete default outcome, not an order the apprentice may issue. Lu-Nanshe may alter the split after reviewing the same source packet. The tablet records her actual decision, not the apprentice's proposal as if it were an order.

### If the window is missed

The gathering proceeds on Day 39 at **reduced scope**, using only resources whose availability is established. From Day 39 through Day 42 the player can complete a **retrospective compilation**: the tablet records the original requests, counted availability, the reduced-scope gathering, and Lu-Nanshe's later disposition. It must not backdate the later compilation or imply that the full gathering was supplied.

After Day 42, the Work remains recoverable through official review of the surviving source records. Lu-Nanshe may accept a qualified retrospective account and close the Work professionally while leaving a physical or institutional shortfall open for follow-up. Missing the window changes the world outcome; it does not erase the Work or deny its one-time Historical Progress XP when accepted.

### Durable world effects

The accepted official disposition creates these durable outcomes:

- **RecordEntry:** `record-era1-eanna-consolidated-summary-tablet` — “Consolidated Summary Tablet: Eanna Gathering.” One immutable RecordEntry preserving obverse source tallies, reverse category totals, the apprentice's proposal, Lu-Nanshe's actual disposition, compiler, sources, and uncertainty limits. Later corrections or supplementary information append or create records; they do not silently rewrite this one.
- **WorldEvent:** `event-era1-eanna-allocation-disposition` — “Lu-Nanshe disposes the Eanna gathering allocation.” The Event records the actual disposition date, Lu-Nanshe as decision-maker, Eanna as institution, and the people/activities affected. It records the official action, not an unobserved physical delivery.
- **Originating InstitutionalMatter:** `matter-era1-eanna-provisioning-undertaking`. The administrative compilation/disposition matter closes when Lu-Nanshe accepts the summary and records her decision. Closure means the assigned administrative task is complete; it does not mean every requested good was supplied.
- **Follow-up InstitutionalMatter:** `matter-era1-eanna-allocation-follow-up`. Create/retain this active matter when Lu-Nanshe defers an unmet quantity, authorizes a carry-forward against Enlil-kisu's expected later barley, or requests a later account. It remains open until a later arrival/receipt or new official disposition supplies grounds to resolve it. If Lu-Nanshe explicitly reduces scope and declines further supply, no carry-forward matter is created; the shortfall remains in the Record and gathering Event.
- **Next career-scale Work:** accepted disposition or accepted recoverable retrospective closure makes the Era I career-scale Work choice eligible. The player chooses one required Work from the approved portfolio alternatives: **The Ordered List** and the second alternative once the portfolio brief resolves it between **The Long Delivery** and **The Work Group Account**. The unchosen alternative is not a declined obligation. Optional Assignments, Challenges, and shortfall follow-up do not gate this unlock.

The player's assigned Work is compilation and submission. Institutional allocation, physical distribution, later arrival, and verification remain separate Events/Records with their actual actors and dates. Final submission does not retroactively invent execution.

## 9. Outcomes and evaluation
## 9. Outcomes and evaluation

Evaluation rewards the quality of the compilation and the appropriateness of the apprentice's proposal, not whether the player guessed a culprit or selected the institution's preferred beneficiary.

Assess separately:
- source tallies accurately transcribed and attributed;
- written records distinguished from spoken claims and assurances;
- correct handling of category-specific units and numerical contexts;
- totals consolidated only where comparison is valid;
- shortfall shown without concealing it or assigning unsupported blame;
- provisional proposal clearly distinguished from an authorized allocation;
- official disposition recorded without implying the apprentice made it;
- final tablet complete, legible, and usable.

A careful player can submit an accurate tablet that leaves a shortfall unresolved. An official may choose a different allocation from the apprentice's proposal without making the apprentice's record inaccurate. A later delivery may prove an assurance false; that consequence must not retroactively make the original source record dishonest.

## 10. Historical grounding and confidence

This scenario uses the existing Era I research base and does not wait for a new broad research cycle.

- **ATTESTED:** Eanna is a major Late Uruk administrative/cultic precinct associated with extensive archaic administrative material. The corpus includes commodity accounts, numerical-only tablets, columns/subtotals/totals, sealings, and other administrative devices.
- **ATTESTED:** Archaic accounts include cereal/grain products, malt, bread/beer contexts, labor groups, field/area records, dairy and fish categories, and other commodities. Proto-cuneiform uses multiple context-dependent numerical/metrological systems.
- **ATTESTED:** MSVO 3, 51/52/55 concern grain products/groats and malt, totals/subtotals, and distinct numerical notations; some readings and destinations remain uncertain.
- **ATTESTED:** W 9827 records a laborer-group account; W 15897,c8 separately records grain disbursement to/consumed as rations by high-ranking officials.
- **INFERRED:** Related source tallies may be compared and compiled for an administrative purpose, provided their units/categories and provenance remain distinct.
- **RECONSTRUCTION:** This gathering, its seasonal window, the particular source packet, the combination of named participants, the Summary Tablet's two-view layout, the four stages, the provisional allocation, and the official disposition are authored gameplay.
- **UNSUPPORTED / EXCLUDE:** A fixed annual Eanna festival date; a claim that W 9827 and W 15897,c8 concern the same group or ration entitlement; an invented groats-to-malt or labor-to-food conversion; a claim that the exact Summary Tablet format is an attested standardized procedure; an apprentice's Day 1 authority or personal seal; any inference of guilt from a shortfall.

The scenario must not imply that every household or work group was directly controlled by Eanna, or that Eanna was a single modern department. A source's commodity, unit, and numerical system must be verified at the point of use.
