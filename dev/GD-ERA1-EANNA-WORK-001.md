# GD-ERA1-EANNA-WORK-001 — The Eanna Work: Scenario Sheet

**Status:** PROPOSED — CONTENT DRAFT FOR GAME DIRECTOR REVIEW  
**Authority:** Game Director direction, 25 November 2026; GD-ERA1-TABLET-WORKSPACE-001  
**Activity layer:** Work (not an Assignment, Challenge, or tutorial)  
**Setting:** Eanna precinct, Uruk, c. 3200 BCE; Late Uruk / Uruk IV–III transition  
**Scope:** Four-stage narrative and player-work outline. Not a technical contract, final tablet transcription, or claim that the exact reconstructed procedure is historically attested.

## 1. Work premise

The Eanna Work asks the apprentice to compile separate precinct/household tallies into a consolidated account that can support an allocation decision. The work is substantial because several distinct source tallies must be kept legible, their categories and units must be compared correctly, and the resulting shortfall must be disposed of without turning uncertainty into an accusation.

The apprentice is responsible for careful compilation, checking, and submission. The apprentice does not possess office, formal authority, or a personal seal at Day 1. The authorized official retains acceptance/disposition authority and applies the institutional seal impression when the Work is accepted or disposed of.

**Historical confidence:** Eanna is a major, well-documented Late Uruk administrative/cultic precinct associated with extensive archaic administrative material. The exact compilation exercise, named participants in this authored interaction, allocation problem, and four-stage player sequence are a **playable reconstruction**, not a documented historical event or a claim about a standardized Eanna workflow.

## 2. Core design rules

- The two-view Summary Tablet workspace is exclusive to **The Eanna Work**. Routine Assignments remain lightweight and snappy.
- Front / Obverse: individual precinct/household tallies, each retaining its own categories and units.
- Back / Reverse: consolidated category totals and the shortfall/allocation disposition.
- Do not aggregate unlike units or silently convert one commodity's numerical system into another.
- The clay tablet remains visible as the work surface. Dialogue appears over or around it.
- Player actions create the accounting entries. Use existing accessible React panel patterns and clay UI tokens `--surface-card` and `--surface-pressed`; lightweight SVG/visual motifs may accompany accessible text.
- This is not a sign-decoding puzzle, canvas-only interaction, custom font/editor, or bespoke art dependency.
- The tablet contains concise accounting information, not ordinary spoken dialogue, multi-clause explanations, legal caveats, or modern report prose.
- A shortfall is not proof of guilt or misconduct. People may make informal arrangements, expect follow-through, or misjudge what will happen.
- Distinguish physical reality, the administrative record, and what the institution understands from that record.
- The apprentice compiles, checks, and submits. An authorized official accepts/disposes and applies the institutional seal impression. The apprentice's **Submit** and the official's **Seal/acceptance** are distinct actions and authorities.

## 3. Four-stage scenario

### Stage 1 — Receive and sort the source tallies

**Purpose:** Establish the scope of the compilation and identify what the separate source records actually say.

**Player sees:**
- The separate precinct/household tallies supplied for the Work.
- Their commodity/category entries and recorded quantities.
- The source identity or grouping needed to keep each tally distinct.
- Any source condition or uncertainty already established by the authored records.

**Player actions:**
- Open and inspect each source tally.
- Identify the relevant categories and units.
- Group source entries for compilation without erasing their individual provenance.
- Ask for clarification only where the authored task makes it available; do not turn the stage into an investigation.

**Dialogue direction:**
- The supervising official explains what must be compiled and why the totals are needed.
- Household/precinct representatives may explain a local category or complain about how their tally is represented.
- Ambient comments may occur without requiring a response.

**Record principle:** A source tally establishes what that record states. It does not by itself prove that the quantity was physically present, delivered, or independently verified.

**Stage output:** The player has a source-by-source working set ready to enter on the tablet's front.

### Stage 2 — Compile the front / obverse

**Purpose:** Create the individual tally side of the Consolidated Summary Tablet.

**Player actions:**
- Select a source precinct/household tally.
- Enter its commodity/category and quantity into the corresponding area.
- Preserve the source's own unit and numerical context.
- Repeat for the remaining supplied tallies.
- Review the front for omissions, transpositions, and accidental mixing of units.

Each player entry visibly adds a mark or simple visual motif to the clay surface. Every mark is paired with readable accessible text. The player does not draw ancient signs by hand and does not need to decode the visual motif.

**Dialogue direction:**
- A person may interrupt with a correction, qualification, or assurance.
- A disagreement about a source tally is presented as a human exchange; it does not silently rewrite the source record.
- Ambient dialogue remains optional and non-blocking.

**Record principle:** The front preserves the distinct source tallies. It is not a place to write explanations about why someone believes a count is right or wrong.

**Stage output:** Obverse/front entries are complete and reviewable.

### Stage 3 — Consolidate the reverse and decide the shortfall disposition

**Purpose:** Calculate compatible category totals and make the one allocation/disposition decision required by the Work.

**Player actions:**
- Turn to the reverse/back view.
- Consolidate entries only where category and units are comparable.
- Keep incompatible units/categories separate rather than forcing a false grand total.
- Compare the consolidated totals with the amount available for the intended allocation.
- Where a shortfall exists, choose the permitted allocation/disposition within the apprentice's assigned task.

**Decision framing:**
The decision concerns how the institution should account for and allocate what is available. It does not ask the apprentice to determine guilt.

The authored choice set should express the actual institutional alternatives in the scenario. A possible structure is:
- allocate according to the established priority/order, leaving a visible shortfall against the remaining need; or
- accept an authorized official's assurance/carry-forward instruction and record that administrative treatment, while preserving that the physical quantity has not thereby increased.

Do not present these as final mechanics until the scenario's specific source quantities, institutional instruction, and authority are authored. Do not offer a choice that lets the apprentice independently authorize a policy or release goods beyond their role.

**Dialogue direction:**
- The responsible official explains the immediate need and the limits of available supply.
- A representative argues for their household/precinct's share.
- Another person may offer an assurance that something will be supplied later or has been set aside. This is a claim/assurance, not a physical count.
- The PC may accept or decline the assurance only if the authorized task permits that judgment.

**Record principle:** The reverse contains consolidated category totals and a concise shortfall/allocation disposition. Spoken dialogue carries competing reasons, pressure, and explanation. A carry-forward may settle an administrative treatment without changing physical reality.

**Stage output:** Reverse entries and the allocation/shortfall disposition are complete.

### Stage 4 — Check, submit, and receive institutional disposition

**Purpose:** Finish the apprentice's work and pass it to the person with authority to accept or dispose of it.

**Player actions:**
- Review both tablet views.
- Check that source tallies remain distinct, compatible totals are correct, and the shortfall disposition matches the player's decision.
- Identify the apprentice as compiler in the authored, historically appropriate way if the scenario requires it.
- Submit the tablet to the authorized official.

**Official action:**
- The authorized official (the scenario's assigned authority, e.g. Lu-Nanshe / Shuruppak-ilu) performs the acceptance or disposition.
- The official applies the institutional seal impression as part of the authorized closeout, where this scenario's reviewed procedure uses that impression.
- The official may accept the compilation, request a correction, or leave a matter unresolved. The apprentice does not apply the institutional seal.

**Outcome distinctions:**
- **Submitted:** the apprentice has completed and handed over the compilation.
- **Accepted / disposed:** the authorized official has acted on the submitted Work.
- **Institutionally sealed:** the official's authorized closeout is represented by the institutional seal impression.
- **World state:** any physical shortage, future delivery, or unresolved uncertainty remains whatever the underlying events establish.

**Stage output:** The Work reaches its authored disposition. The final record is not silently rewritten; a later correction or supplementary record, if authored, preserves the earlier submission and official action.

## 4. Tablet content contract

### Front / Obverse
- Individual precinct/household tallies remain separate.
- Each entry includes only the concise category/commodity and quantity information needed for the account.
- Each source retains its unit and provenance.
- Do not write dialogue, justifications, accusations, or legal clauses onto the clay.

### Back / Reverse
- Consolidated category totals where comparison is valid.
- Separate totals where units or numerical systems are incompatible.
- Amount available for the intended allocation, if established by the authored source state.
- Shortfall and allocation disposition in concise accounting form.

The exact sign shapes, numerical notation, categories, units, source names, and quantities must be drawn from the existing authored Era I content and research. This sheet intentionally does not invent them. The player-facing interface must always provide accessible modern-language labels for the entries.

## 6. Outcomes and evaluation

Evaluation should reward the quality of the compilation and the appropriateness of the apprentice's judgment, not whether the player guessed a hidden culprit or chose the institution's preferred beneficiary.

Relevant evaluation dimensions:
- source tallies accurately transcribed and kept distinct;
- correct handling of category-specific units and numerical context;
- totals correctly consolidated only where comparison is valid;
- shortfall represented without concealing it or assigning unsupported blame;
- allocation/disposition follows the instruction and authority actually given;
- assurances remain attributed and do not become physical facts;
- tablet submitted clearly and completely.

A careful player can produce an accurate record that still contains an unresolved shortfall. A player may accept an assurance that later proves false; the consequence should arise from that choice and the world's subsequent events, not from retroactively declaring the original record dishonest. An error may be corrected before submission or addressed through a later authorized correction, but the committed record must not be silently rewritten.

## 7. Historical grounding and confidence

This scenario uses the existing Era I research base and does not wait for a new research cycle.

- **ATTESTED:** Eanna is a major Late Uruk administrative/cultic precinct associated with extensive archaic administrative material. The corpus includes commodity accounts, numerical-only tablets, entries with columns/subtotals/totals, sealings, and other administrative devices.
- **ATTESTED:** Early writing includes structured administrative records and lexical lists; ordinary spoken dialogue and multi-clause narrative/legal prose do not belong on the Uruk IV accounting tablet as represented here.
- **ATTESTED:** Proto-cuneiform used multiple numerical/metrological systems; the relevant system depends on the commodity or quantity type. Distinct units must not be treated as interchangeable.
- **ATTESTED:** Cylinder seals and clay sealings participated in systems of custody, access, and administrative control. Their function is broader than a modern signature.
- **INFERRED:** Compiling related tallies and comparing category totals is a defensible administrative activity within this broad setting.
- **RECONSTRUCTION:** This exact Eanna Work, its four stages, the consolidated two-view artifact, its shortfall/allocation decision, its named dialogue participants, and the official's particular disposition are authored gameplay.
- **UNSUPPORTED / EXCLUDE:** A claim that this exact tablet layout or four-stage workflow is an attested standard Eanna procedure; a Day 1 apprentice's possession of a personal seal or authority to apply an institutional seal; any universal household unit or numerical system; any claim that a shortage proves theft or negligence.

The exact authored commodity set and values should use existing researched Era I material and the scenario's source-state data. If those data do not support a common total, keep the categories separate rather than inventing a conversion.
