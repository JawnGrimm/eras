# GD-ERA1-EANNA-WORK-001 — The Eanna Work: Scenario Sheet

**Status:** PROPOSED — REVISED CONTENT DRAFT FOR GAME DIRECTOR REVIEW  
**Authority:** Game Director direction, 25 November 2026; GD-ERA1-TABLET-WORKSPACE-001  
**Activity layer:** Required Era I milestone Work (not an Assignment, Challenge, or tutorial)  
**Setting:** Eanna precinct, Uruk, c. 3200 BCE; Late Uruk / Uruk IV–III transition  
**Scope:** Four-stage authored scenario proposal. Not a technical contract, final tablet transcription, or claim that this exact procedure is historically attested.

## 1. Work premise and scale

The Eanna Work is a milestone undertaking within the wider **Eanna Provisioning Undertaking**. Its immediate deliverable is a Consolidated Summary Tablet: separate household/precinct source tallies on the obverse, compatible category totals and a provisional shortfall treatment on the reverse, then an authorized institutional disposition.

It is not a longer stock audit. The Work asks the apprentice to make a reliable account out of material that does not all have the same status: written tallies, copied entries, a labor count, and spoken requests or assurances. The player must decide what can be carried onto the tablet, what must remain separate, and what cannot yet be counted. The official—not the apprentice—decides the allocation.

The approaching Eanna gathering gives the work consequence and a date window. The gathering and its timing are an **authored reconstruction**: the current research supports Eanna as a setting for large-scale ceremonial/public gatherings and provisioning activity, but does not establish this exact gathering, a fixed annual festival calendar, or a precise historical date. The scenario should describe it as a local gathering expected before the current seasonal work window closes, not name an unattested festival.

The apprentice begins without office, formal authority, or a personal seal. They compile, check, and submit. The authorized official reviews the proposal, decides whether to ratify or alter it, and applies the institutional seal impression where the authored closeout uses one.

**Historical confidence:** Eanna is a major, well-documented Late Uruk administrative/cultic precinct associated with extensive archaic administrative material. The exact gathering, named participants' involvement in this particular undertaking, source packet, consolidated two-view tablet, allocation problem, and four-stage sequence are playable reconstruction—not a documented historical event or standardized Eanna procedure.

## 2. Core design rules

- The two-view Summary Tablet workspace is exclusive to **The Eanna Work**. Routine Assignments remain lightweight and snappy.
- Front / Obverse: individual household/precinct source tallies, retaining their source identity, commodity/category, quantity, and own unit/notation.
- Back / Reverse: consolidated category totals only where comparison is valid; otherwise keep categories separate. Include the available amount and provisional shortfall treatment only where the source state establishes them.
- Do not aggregate unlike commodities, numerical systems, or labor counts into a false grand total.
- The clay tablet remains visible as the work surface. Dialogue appears over or around it. Player actions create the marks.
- Use existing accessible React panel patterns and clay UI tokens `--surface-card` and `--surface-pressed`; lightweight SVG/visual motifs may accompany accessible text.
- No bespoke sign-decoding engine, custom font/editor, canvas-only interaction, unreadable symbol quiz, new art dependency, or wireframe blocker.
- The tablet itself carries concise accounting marks—categories, quantities, totals, and appropriate seal impression—not spoken arguments, multi-clause explanations, legal caveats, or modern report prose.
- A shortfall is not proof of guilt or misconduct. An assurance is not a physical count. A request is not an entitlement. A sealing is not proof that the holder had authority to commit a load.
- Distinguish physical reality, the source Record, the new Summary Tablet, and what each person or institution knows.
- The apprentice's provisional allocation layout is a recommendation for review, not an order or authorization. Only the appointed official makes the institutional disposition.

It must not pretend that the values below are literal transcriptions of one excavated tablet or that the source records are historically linked.

Until that source is authorized, keep the third claim qualitative and outside the numerical total.

### Non-numeric claim that must remain separate

Geme-Inana's existing household context includes a request for timber for work-frames and an earlier outbound exchange whose return is incomplete. The existing Two Claims, One Load content also establishes a separate Storehouse timber request, a textile-household timber request, a small timber delivery receipt, and Kuli's reported account. That scenario is a separate authored matter; do not silently import its timber load into this Work as if it were the same gathering's stock. It can inform Geme-Inana's voice and motivation, but a Work-specific timber quantity requires its own source-state entry.

### Stage 1 sorting rule

The player sorts material into three working groups before entering the tablet:

1. **Written tally / copied record:** eligible for transcription with its source and unit preserved.
2. **Separate count or category:** eligible for its own obverse entry only if the Work's source packet explicitly includes it; it is not automatically linked to a commodity issue.
3. **Spoken request, expectation, or assurance:** dialogue context only. It does not become a tablet quantity unless an independently identified source or physical count supports it.

This is the distinct judgment in Stage 1. It prevents Stage 2 from becoming a second pass through the same material and prevents oral pressure from silently becoming accounting evidence.

## 5. Four-stage playable scenario

### Stage 1 — Sort the sources and establish what can be counted

**Purpose:** Establish the scope of the compilation and distinguish record-backed quantities from separate counts and spoken claims.

**Player sees:**
- The source tallies and copies selected for the gathering-specific packet.
- The associated person and institutional context for each source.
- Spoken requests and assurances from the named participants.
- The gathering's authored seasonal window and what becomes harder if it passes.

**Player actions:**
- Sort each item as a written tally/copy, separate count/category, or spoken claim/assurance.
- Identify which entries can be carried forward and which must remain separate.
- Identify incompatible commodity categories or numerical contexts before calculation.
- Preserve the source identity and provenance; do not promote an oral assurance to a recorded quantity.

**Dialogue:**
- Shuruppak-ilu explains that the task is to prepare a usable account, not decide who deserves goods.
- Ur-Nanna distinguishes a storehouse count from a statement about goods expected later.
- Nin-kasi and Geme-Inana each explain what delay means for their work.
- Enlil-kisu distinguishes what he dispatched or reported from what has actually arrived.
- Lu-Nanshe makes clear that the priority decision remains hers.

**Stage output:** A source-sorted working set. No tablet quantity is created merely because someone made a request.

### Stage 2 — Compile the obverse / front

**Purpose:** Enter the separate source tallies without erasing their origins or units.

**Player actions:**
- Select each eligible source tally and enter its commodity/category and quantity.
- Preserve each source's own unit and numerical context.
- Keep separate household/precinct entries distinct even when they concern related goods.
- Review for omissions, transpositions, or accidental mixing of units.

Each player entry visibly adds a mark or lightweight visual motif to the clay surface. Every mark is paired with readable accessible text. The player does not draw ancient signs by hand or decode the visual motif.

**Dialogue:**
- Nin-kasi may correct how a category is described, but her statement does not silently change the source tally.
- Enlil-kisu may clarify whether he is describing dispatch, expectation, or receipt.
- Geme-Inana may press for the timber need to be considered; the request remains separate unless a Work-specific quantity record supports it.

**Stage output:** Obverse/front entries are complete, attributable, and reviewable.

### Stage 3 — Consolidate the reverse and prepare a provisional allocation

**Purpose:** Calculate compatible totals and lay out a proposed treatment for the shortfall for Lu-Nanshe's review.

**Player actions:**
- Turn to the reverse/back view.
- Consolidate only entries whose category and units are demonstrably comparable.
- Keep groats, malt, labor counts, timber requests, and any other unlike categories separate.
- Compare supported totals with the amount physically available, but only where the source packet establishes that amount.
- Prepare one provisional allocation layout or record the shortfall without proposing a distribution where the evidence or instruction does not support one.
- Attribute any carry-forward or expected delivery to the person/source that made the claim; do not add it to physical stock.

**The apprentice's decision:** Which supported figures and proposed treatment should be placed before Lu-Nanshe? The player may recommend a priority layout, a reduced scope, or an explicitly unresolved shortfall. The player is not choosing an enforceable allocation.

**Official authority:** Lu-Nanshe may ratify the proposal, change the priority, defer a portion, or reject the proposal. She alone makes the institutional allocation decision in this scenario. Shuruppak-ilu supervises the record and may explain why a proposal exceeds the evidence, but he does not choose the beneficiary.

**Dialogue:**
- Nin-kasi argues that a late or reduced allocation will affect the work already underway.
- Geme-Inana points out that timber delayed beyond the seasonal window leaves the work-frames idle.
- Ur-Nanna states what is available under the storehouse record, without treating a request as an entitlement.
- Lu-Nanshe asks for the shortfall to remain visible rather than hidden in a total.

**Stage output:** A provisional Reverse layout for official review. It is not an order, release instruction, or completed allocation.

### Stage 4 — Check, submit, and receive institutional disposition

**Purpose:** Complete the apprentice's record and pass the allocation decision to the authorized official.

**Player actions:**
- Review both tablet views.
- Check source tallies, unit separation, compatible totals, and the provisional allocation/shortfall notation.
- Identify the apprentice as compiler in the authored, historically appropriate way if required.
- Submit the tablet to Lu-Nanshe for disposition.

**Official action:**
- Lu-Nanshe reviews the source distinctions and provisional layout.
- She may ratify it, alter the priority, defer part of the distribution, or return it for correction.
- If the scenario's closeout uses an institutional seal impression, Lu-Nanshe applies it after her decision. The apprentice never applies the seal.
- Shuruppak-ilu may explain the assessment and recordkeeping lesson, but does not substitute his judgment for Lu-Nanshe's allocation authority.

**Outcome distinctions:**
- **Submitted:** the apprentice has completed and handed over the compilation.
- **Accepted / disposed:** Lu-Nanshe has made the institutional decision.
- **Institutionally sealed:** the authorized closeout is represented by the appropriate institutional seal impression, if used in this scenario.
- **Physical state:** the amount actually available, any delivery not yet received, and any unmet need remain as the underlying events establish. A carry-forward does not create stock.

**Stage output:** A committed Summary Tablet and official disposition. Later corrections or supplementary records preserve the earlier submission and disposition; they do not silently rewrite them.

## 7. Seasonal window and consequences

**Proposed window:** the gathering is expected before the current seasonal work window closes. Use a visible in-game date/window established by the authored Era I calendar; do not claim that the exact date or named gathering is historically attested. The Work can span more than one Workday. It is not a real-time timer and does not consume all six AP automatically.

**If the window is met:** the official's disposition can guide the gathering's reduced or full preparation. The player does not personally cause the gathering or claim credit for a historical event.

**If the window is missed:** the world does not pretend the date never passed. The institution uses a catch-up route: the original gathering proceeds at a reduced scope or the outstanding allocation is carried to a later institutional need. The exact branch must be authored with the final source-state quantities; missing the date does not erase the Work or block historical progression.

**Durable world effects proposed for this Work:**
- One immutable **Consolidated Summary Tablet Record** preserving the source references, obverse entries, reverse totals, compiler, submission, and official disposition.
- One objective **Work disposition Event** identifying the official action and the people/institution who received it.
- The originating Eanna provisioning matter is updated according to the official disposition.
- If an allocation remains outstanding or a carry-forward is authorized, a linked follow-up matter remains active. A later Assignment may be offered to verify an actual arrival/receipt or report on the deferred need; it must not be generated merely because someone made an unsupported promise.
- The next required career-scale Work invitation becomes eligible only after the milestone reaches its accepted completion or authored recoverable closure, consistent with the approved Era I Works portfolio brief.

These are domain-level outcome requirements, not a technical schema or authorization to modify runtime code. Stage effects must be committed when they occur, with actor and source provenance; final submission must not retroactively invent earlier events.

## 9. Outcomes and evaluation

Evaluation rewards the quality of the compilation and the appropriateness of the apprentice's proposal, not whether the player guessed a culprit or selected the institution's preferred beneficiary.

Assess separately:
- source tallies accurately transcribed and attributed;
- written records distinguished from spoken claims and assurances;
- correct handling of category-specific units and numerical contexts;
- totals consolidated only where comparison is valid;
- shortfall shown without concealing it or assigning unsupported blame;
- provisional proposal clearly distinguished from an authorized allocation;
- official disposition recorded without implying the apprentice made it;
- final tablet complete, legible, and usable.

A careful player can submit an accurate tablet that leaves a shortfall unresolved. An official may choose a different allocation from the apprentice's proposal without making the apprentice's record inaccurate. A later delivery may prove an assurance false; that consequence must not retroactively make the original source record dishonest.

## 10. Historical grounding and confidence

This scenario uses the existing Era I research base and does not wait for a new broad research cycle.

- **ATTESTED:** Eanna is a major Late Uruk administrative/cultic precinct associated with extensive archaic administrative material. The corpus includes commodity accounts, numerical-only tablets, columns/subtotals/totals, sealings, and other administrative devices.
- **ATTESTED:** Archaic accounts include cereal/grain products, malt, bread/beer contexts, labor groups, field/area records, dairy and fish categories, and other commodities. Proto-cuneiform uses multiple context-dependent numerical/metrological systems.
- **ATTESTED:** MSVO 3, 51/52/55 concern grain products/groats and malt, totals/subtotals, and distinct numerical notations; some readings and destinations remain uncertain.
- **ATTESTED:** W 9827 records a laborer-group account; W 15897,c8 separately records grain disbursement to/consumed as rations by high-ranking officials.
- **INFERRED:** Related source tallies may be compared and compiled for an administrative purpose, provided their units/categories and provenance remain distinct.
- **RECONSTRUCTION:** This gathering, its seasonal window, the particular source packet, the combination of named participants, the Summary Tablet's two-view layout, the four stages, the provisional allocation, and the official disposition are authored gameplay.
- **UNSUPPORTED / EXCLUDE:** A fixed annual Eanna festival date; a claim that W 9827 and W 15897,c8 concern the same group or ration entitlement; an invented groats-to-malt or labor-to-food conversion; a claim that the exact Summary Tablet format is an attested standardized procedure; an apprentice's Day 1 authority or personal seal; any inference of guilt from a shortfall.

The scenario must not imply that every household or work group was directly controlled by Eanna, or that Eanna was a single modern department. A source's commodity, unit, and numerical system must be verified at the point of use.
